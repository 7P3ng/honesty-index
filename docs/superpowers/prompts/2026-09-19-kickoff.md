## Levers applied (Opus 5, interactive)

- **Gate stated mechanically** ("ONE section per message … wait for my explicit yes") —
  Opus 5 will otherwise present all sections at once and start building.
- **Scope clause + finish-whole-task clause** verbatim from the Opus 5 migration guidance —
  it expands scope and reports early "done" without it.
- **No verification scaffolding** — Opus 5 self-verifies; "double-check" wording causes
  over-verification. The only verification language is the project's own prove-fail rule.
- **Delegation capped, not encouraged** — Opus 5 over-delegates (reverse of 4.8); the box's
  model-guard hook still requires `model=`.
- **Deliverable-length calibration** — Opus 5 pads Markdown files.
- **Human-only steps named with their trigger** ("raise at the moment they block you") so
  the session doesn't front-load a setup checklist Thomas can't act on yet.
- **No wall-clock anchors, no skill names beyond the two it must invoke** — a named skill
  in a brief can cause runaway invocation; brainstorming and writing-plans are the path.
